# Telinha release notes
## Features

1. The app updates itself: it finds a new release, downloads it in the background and asks to restart.
2. A share now checks its own audio: if Telinha's sound ever reaches the viewers, the computer audio is cut and the app says so.
3. Exclusive fullscreen games can be shared as a window.

## Improvements

1. Call tiles keep their 16:9 shape and stay centered when the window is resized.
2. The settings button is now a gear.
3. The portable app is named simply Telinha.exe.
4. The settings window shows the app version.

## Fixes

1. Viewers no longer hear the call voices in a share when the app file is named Telinha.exe.
2. A share goes out without computer audio, instead of leaking the call voices, when Telinha cannot be left out of it.

Notes:
Builds older than this one do not update themselves. Download this release by hand once, and the next ones arrive on their own.
