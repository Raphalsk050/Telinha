# Telinha release notes
## Features

1. More choices for the quality of a stream, in Personalizada: a most rate from 1 to 50 Mbps or a typed value from 0.5 to 100 Mbps, the kind of compression (automatic, variable rate or fixed quality), and what gives way when the internet is short (keep the picture sharp or keep it smooth). Advanced options set how often a full picture is sent and turn low latency off. Each choice shows what it costs to send for each person watching, and warns when it is more than your internet carries now.
2. Two new presets for streams: Máxima qualidade, for a local network, and Internet fraca.
3. A button that looks for a new version at once, in Configurações, Atualizações. It says whether you already have the newest one, or what went wrong.

## Improvements

1. The settings are now pages in a sidebar: Perfil, Voz e vídeo, Aparência, Mensagens and Atualizações.
2. The quality chosen for a stream is remembered for the next one.
3. The automatic quality of a stream stays at most 40 Mbps for each person watching, even on a local network that carries more.

## Fixes

1. The right button on a stream or a camera open in full screen did nothing. It now opens the menu of the person.
2. A stream open in full screen blurred for an instant now and then, more often while the menu of the person was in use. The picture was sometimes made at the size of the small tile behind it.
