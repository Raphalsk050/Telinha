# Telinha release notes
## Features

1. Threads, as in Slack: hover a message and choose "Responder em tópico" to answer it in a panel beside the conversation. The answers stay out of the channel, and the message shows how many answers it has, who answered and when the last one came.
2. Pinned messages: pin any message from its toolbar or its menu, for everyone in the conversation. The pin in the header lists them; click one to jump to it.
3. A new profile editor: the form at the left and a live preview of the card at the right, pronouns, a counter for "Sobre mim", and changes kept until "Salvar alterações". Trying to leave with changes not saved shakes the bar, as Discord.
4. Voice channels open as a full stage, as Discord: the call takes the whole page and its chat opens beside it from the header. Joining a call, the buttons pour out of one pill and pull apart like drops.
5. Input sensitivity (Settings > Voz e vídeo): automatic, or a threshold you drag on the live meter. Only what passes it is sent, and the green ring around your picture lights exactly when your voice goes out.
6. Nudges, as MSN had: the bell in a direct conversation shakes the other person's window with a rattle.
7. GIF links play in the chat, and YouTube, Vimeo, TikTok, Twitch clips, Streamable, Dailymotion and video file links play inside the conversation.
8. Pick the app icon in Settings > Aparência.

## Improvements

1. Hover and state changes of buttons, menus, switches and rows ease in and out instead of jumping.
2. The hang-up button looks like the other round buttons until the pointer is on it, then glows red.
3. Settings have more room and line up as Discord's, with sliders in tidy rows and choices as switches.
4. The voice panel and the user panel at the foot of the sidebar float together in one card.
5. Three people in a call show as two on top and one below, filling the stage.
6. Pictures, videos and link previews shrink to fit narrow columns instead of being cut.

## Fixes

None.
