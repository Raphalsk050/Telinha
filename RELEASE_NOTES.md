# Telinha release notes
## Features

1. Notices inside the app: while Telinha is in front, a message from another conversation, a mention of your name, a call or a screen share that starts in your room arrives as a card at the top right. Reply from the card, or open the conversation with a click; it goes away after 8 seconds, and waits while the mouse is over it.
2. Sound effects volume (Settings > Voz e vídeo > Efeitos sonoros): from silent to twice as loud, for the join, leave, mute, message and call sounds. A sound plays when you let go of the slider.

## Improvements

1. An incoming call rings as a card at the top right with Recusar and Atender, instead of a dialog in the middle of the window.

## Fixes

1. Keyboard shortcuts work while Telinha is in front too, including with the shortcuts page open.
2. Numeric keypad keys record and work as themselves ("NUM 2", "NUM ENTER") with Num Lock on or off, instead of turning into arrows.
3. Keys that Windows only reports when let go, such as Print Screen, can be recorded and used.
4. The message box no longer runs under the member list in the glass theme.
